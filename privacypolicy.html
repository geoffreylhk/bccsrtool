Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

bccsrtool.ca is committed to being transparent about how this site works.

What Data Is Collected

This tool does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Assessment inputs, including land use selections, contaminant choices, and concentration values, are processed within your browser and are not sent to a server.

To support resume and navigation features, progress may be saved locally in your browser using browser storage. This local progress stays on your device and can be cleared by starting a new assessment, clearing your browser storage, or using browser privacy controls.

Analytics

This site uses basic anonymous analytics to understand how the tool is being used, for example which pages are visited most often. No personally identifiable information is collected. IP addresses are anonymized.

If you prefer not to be included in analytics, you can disable JavaScript or use a browser extension that blocks analytics tracking.

Cookies and Local Storage

This site uses no advertising cookies and no tracking cookies. Browser storage may be used only to remember assessment progress locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server access logs as part of its hosting infrastructure. For details, refer to GitHub's privacy policy at github.com/site/privacy.

Your Rights

Because no personal data is collected or stored by bccsrtool.ca, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete from a site database. If you want to remove locally saved assessment progress, clear your browser storage for this site or start a new assessment.

Contact

For questions about this tool or these policies, contact Geoffrey Lee through LinkedIn.

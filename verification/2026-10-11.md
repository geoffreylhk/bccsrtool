## Threshold values and provenance

Official sources retrieved on 11 October 2026:

- Regulation contents: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/375_96_00
- Schedule 3.1 Part 1: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/133_2022_Sch%203.1-part1
- Schedule 3.1 Part 2: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/159_2025_Sch%203.1-part2
- Schedule 3.2 water: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/2_2023_Sch%203.2

The official consolidation is current to 6 October 2026 and reports an overall last amendment of 1 July 2026. The dataset label is 27 October 2025. A newer overall amendment is not, by itself, proof that every numerical standard changed; actual table entries were compared.

The accompanying threshold-source-check.csv audits all 640 stored entries, mapping substance, land use, matrix, source matrix number, source column and PDF page. Parsed cells were compared using their spatial position in the official table; special pH dependency was checked as a notation, not as a implemented numeric-band calculation. Relevant source pages were visually inspected, including benzene, chromium, PCE and TCE.

All 320 soil fields match the Schedule 3.1 Part 1 HUMAN HEALTH PROTECTION intake-of-contaminated-soil row, including NS and >1000 mg/g special notation. This establishes transcription provenance for that particular row, not the applicable controlling standard for a real site. Environmental pathways and site-specific factors can impose other standards.

The 320 gw fields are not a validated water dataset. Most scalar values and pH placeholders follow Schedule 3.1 Part 1 SOIL standards for protecting drinking groundwater. Part 1 explicitly defines its values in ug/g unless otherwise stated. The app instead compares water measurements in ug/L to these values. Dimensional conversion between soil mass concentration and water concentration is not valid.

Example: benzene's stored 0.035 is a soil drinking-groundwater-protection standard in ug/g (Part 1 page 4). Schedule 3.2's drinking-water standard is 5 ug/L (water PDF page 3). Schedule 3.2 also contains distinct aquatic-life, irrigation and livestock columns; drinking water cannot be silently assumed for every site.

Additional provenance defects:

- PCE: all eight stored gw values of 2.5 come from the soil row protecting aquatic surface water (Part 1 page 42), not the drinking-groundwater soil row, which is blank. Its Schedule 3.2 drinking-water value is 30 ug/L.
- TCE: stored gw values mostly resemble soil invertebrate/plant toxicity values (Part 1 page 44), rather than any water row. The agricultural field is 1.5 versus 15 in that soil-toxicity row, and industrial is null versus 25. Its Schedule 3.2 drinking-water value is 5 ug/L. At RLLD, entering 10 ug/L currently reports Below against 15, even though 10 exceeds the drinking-water standard of 5 if that pathway applies.
- Chromium: the dataset marks gw pH-dependent, but the referenced soil table distinguishes hexavalent and trivalent chromium (Part 1 pages 11-12). This is species dependence, not pH dependence.
- Dioxins/furans: the matched soil row requires TCDD toxicity equivalents with the specified equivalency factors (Part 1 pages 37 onward). An unweighted total concentration is not interchangeable with TEQ. The app's Total label does not explain this requirement.
- PFOS: Schedule 3.1 Part 1 page 34 limits these standards to specified Schedule 2 industrial/commercial activities (A4, C3, E10 or G1). The app does not collect those activity conditions.
- PCBs: Part 1 page 36 defines total soil PCBs as the specified sum of Aroclors and requires dioxin-like PCBs to be evaluated with dioxins/furans. The generic Total label does not convey these rules.
- Unrestricted: stored strings replace the source notation >1000 mg/g. Numerical notation matching does not establish that a site has no regulatory restrictions.

The current exported data.json field path identifies an internal dataset location, not regulatory provenance. A complete provenance record needs schedule/part, matrix or substance row, land-use or water-use column, pathway, units, relevant footnotes and source version.

bccsrtool.ca is an independent educational project built to make BC Contaminated Sites Regulation (CSR) Schedule 3.1 screening concepts more accessible. It is not a professional consulting service.

This tool does not replace professional advice.

Results generated by this tool do not constitute a formal Phase I or Phase II Environmental Site Assessment (ESA) under the BC Contaminated Sites Regulation.

Threshold values displayed are based on BC CSR Schedule 3.1 as interpreted at the time of last update. Regulations change. Always verify current threshold values against the official BC Laws website before making any regulatory or transactional decision.

This tool screens for human health protection pathways only. Environmental protection pathways are not currently covered.

Site-specific conditions, including soil type, groundwater depth, exposure pathways, and receptor sensitivity, affect whether thresholds apply. This tool cannot account for site-specific conditions.

No professional liability is assumed or implied by the use of this tool.

For any decision involving property transactions, regulatory submissions, remediation planning, or professional practice, always engage a qualified Approved Professional as defined under the BC Environmental Management Act.
